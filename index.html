The 48-Hour Cart Rule

When you crave buying clothes or games, add it to your wishlist jar instead. 70% of dopamine fades after 48 hours, saving you thousands.

Study Habits • 3 min

Active Recall vs. Re-reading

Highlighting notes creates an "illusion of competence." Closing your notebook and reciting answers yields 2.5x better retention during finals.
